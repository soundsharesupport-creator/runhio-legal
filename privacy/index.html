1. In short

  • RUNHIO has no accounts and no RUNHIO servers. Races and sharing happen directly between nearby devices.
  • RUNHIO contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services from other companies.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not track you across apps or websites.
  • Most information stays on your device. It leaves your device only when you take part in a nearby race, share a runner, save or share a photo, or write to support. While RUNHIO is open, your nickname and avatar emoji and colour are also visible to nearby devices (see section 4).

2. Information stored on your device

RUNHIO stores the following in its own storage on your device:

  • your display name and avatar (an emoji and a colour, or a picture you chose); the display name is a nickname you choose and does not need to be your real name; saved avatar pictures are also kept on this device;
  • the runners you draw (lines, colours, joints and names) and unsaved drawing drafts;
  • race history and results, including photo-finish pictures;
  • settings such as appearance, language, sound, haptics, runner control (Button or Phone Tilt) and invitations;
  • the players you chose to hide (their nickname and the random identifier of their RUNHIO install), so you no longer get their race invitations or runners; you can show them again in Settings › Hidden Players;
  • whether RUNHIO Plus was last confirmed as active, so the app can show your plan before StoreKit answers;
  • the RUNHIO Free creation counter (see section 7);
  • a random install identifier kept in this device’s Keychain (see section 4).

If you choose Phone Tilt under Runner Control in Settings, RUNHIO reads the device’s motion sensors only while you race, to turn the tilt of the phone into your runner’s speed. The readings are used on the device straight away; they are not stored and do not leave the device. iOS does not ask for a permission for this. With the Button setting, the motion sensors are not used.

3. Photos

To use a picture as your avatar, you pick it with the system photo picker. RUNHIO receives only the picture you choose. It keeps a local gallery of cropped 512 × 512 pixel copies of each picture you use as an avatar, so you can pick it again later; you can delete these copies in the avatar editor. RUNHIO does not use the camera.

A photo-finish picture is saved to your Photos library only when you tap Save; for this, RUNHIO asks for permission to add photos (not to read your library). When you tap Share, the picture is passed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you choose to send it.

4. Nearby races and sharing (Local Network)

RUNHIO connects nearby devices directly to each other with Apple’s Bonjour and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi to find nearby players, send invitations, run races and share runners. The devices do not need to be on the same Wi-Fi network; if they are, RUNHIO may also connect through it. Local Network permission is required for this.

While RUNHIO is open on screen, it shows your nickname and your avatar's emoji and colour (never an avatar picture) to nearby RUNHIO devices so friends can send you a runner or invite you to a race. This happens even when race invitations are turned off in Settings. It stops when you close RUNHIO or it goes to the background; if race invitations are on, iOS may keep RUNHIO reachable for invitations for a short time after you leave the app. When you host a race, the race name and your nickname are also shown to nearby devices so others can find and join it.

Each name shown to nearby devices begins with a short part of a random install identifier. RUNHIO creates this identifier once and keeps it in this device’s Keychain, so it stays the same even if you delete and reinstall the app. It lets races and sharing reach the right device. It is random, contains no personal data and is not linked to any account.

During a nearby race, RUNHIO exchanges with the other participants’ devices:

  • your nickname and avatar (emoji and colour, and a small copy of your avatar picture if you set one);
  • the runner you race with;
  • your speed input during the race (how much you press the push button or tilt the phone, sent as a number; never the sensor readings themselves);
  • the race name, lobby settings (including the stadium appearance chosen by the host), readiness status, race start, progress, results and leaving the race;
  • random identifiers that keep the race in sync (a random player identifier created by RUNHIO when you first set up your profile; it stays the same between races and is not linked to your identity).

This data goes directly between the devices; it does not pass through a RUNHIO server. RUNHIO does not add its own encryption to race data, so do not put private information in your nickname or race names. Other participants’ devices keep the results of races you ran together, including your nickname and runner, in their own race history.

5. Sharing runners

When you share a runner through AirDrop, Messages, Files or another app, the system sends the runner file you chose. A runner file (.runhio) contains the drawing, its name, when it was created and last changed, and the RUNHIO version that made it.

With nearby sharing, RUNHIO first sends the nearby device you picked a request with your nickname, your avatar’s emoji and colour, and the runner’s name and a small preview picture. The request is not encrypted by RUNHIO. The runner file itself is sent only after the owner of that device accepts, and the file is encrypted with keys created for that one exchange.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications and someone nearby invites you to a race just after you leave RUNHIO, you get a local notification. This works only for a short time (usually up to about half a minute) while iOS still keeps RUNHIO running in the background. After that iOS pauses the app, and invitations reach you only when you open RUNHIO again. There is no push-notification server.

7. RUNHIO Free creation counter

RUNHIO Free includes a limited number of runners you draw and save for the first time. To keep this limit, RUNHIO stores a counter and the identifiers of those runners in the app’s storage and in this device’s Keychain. It is not synced through iCloud, is not used for tracking and is not sent anywhere. Imported and received runners are not counted.

8. RUNHIO Plus

Purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds and Restore Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details. RUNHIO checks on your device whether your Apple Account has an active RUNHIO Plus subscription.

9. Contacting support

If you write to support from the app, your message is sent from your own email app. It includes the subject and text you wrote, and the RUNHIO version, iOS version and device type to help us answer. We use this only to reply to you.

10. Keeping and deleting information

You can delete runners and race history in the app, remove saved avatar pictures in the avatar editor, and change your nickname and avatar at any time. Deleting RUNHIO removes its data from your device; the Free creation counter and the install identifier in the Keychain may remain, even after you reinstall the app, until the device is erased. Copies of race results kept by other participants are on their devices and are not controlled by us.

11. Children

RUNHIO is intended for people who meet the age rating shown for RUNHIO on the App Store and the minimum age required by the laws of their country.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, EEA or UK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because RUNHIO keeps information on your device, most of these actions are available directly in the app. For anything else, write to soundshare.support@gmail.com.

13. Security

Information is kept in app storage protected by iOS. Runner files sent to nearby devices are encrypted; the share request and nearby race data go directly between devices without encryption by RUNHIO. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
