About RUNHIO

RUNHIO lets you draw a creature, rig how it runs on the legs you drew, practice against sample runners, and race with friends nearby over the local network. There is no RUNHIO account and no central game server.

Your profile, runners and race history are stored on this device. Your nickname, avatar and the runner you race with are sent to nearby devices only during a nearby race or when you share a runner; see the Privacy Policy.

Runner control

In a race you set your runner’s pace yourself. Choose how in Settings › Runner Control:

  • Button (default): hold the round button to speed up, let go to run at normal pace.
  • Phone Tilt: your position at the start counts as neutral; tilt the phone away from you to speed up, towards you to slow down.

Speeding up fills the meter above your runner’s name. If it fills up, the runner trips and falls, so ease off now and then to let it drop. If you don’t touch anything, your runner simply runs on its own. In races with friends everyone steers their own runner; bots run by themselves.

Free and Plus

  • RUNHIO Free: up to four runners you draw and save for the first time; saved runners cannot be edited; import and share are free; practice and nearby multiplayer are included.
  • RUNHIO Plus: unlimited creation and editing of runners; same racing and sharing features.

Prices shown in the app come from the App Store (StoreKit). If a price cannot be loaded, purchase stays disabled until StoreKit provides one.

Restore purchases

Open Free / Plus in RUNHIO and tap Restore Purchases. Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restore recovers entitlements Apple already associates with that account; it does not create a new purchase.

Refunds

Refunds for App Store subscriptions are handled by Apple. Request a refund through Apple (for example [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) or Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases → Purchase History). RUNHIO cannot issue App Store refunds directly.

Nearby races and Local Network

If devices cannot find each other, check Wi‑Fi / local network settings and allow Local Network for RUNHIO on every device (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network). Discovery is not guaranteed in every environment.

Hidden players

If someone nearby sends you invitations or runners you don't want, tap ⋮ on their invitation card or on the incoming runner and choose Hide player. You won't get race invitations or runners from them any more. To see them again, open Settings › Hidden Players and tap Show. To report content or behaviour, write to the support address above.

Photo Finish

From Results you can save or share the photo-finish image when one was captured. Saving to Photos uses the system Photos permission only when you ask to save.
